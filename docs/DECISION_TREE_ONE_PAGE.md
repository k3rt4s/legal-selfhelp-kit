# Decision tree, one page

This is a condensation for printing. DECISION_TREE.md is the authority.
Forums, dollar limits, deadlines, and program names are in your own
references/state_XX.md, not here.

1. What state is the lawyer licensed and practicing in?
   Load references/state_XX.md. Do not know the state: find out before continuing.

2. Money missing from a trust account, or retainer taken and no work at all?
   Yes: disciplinary authority (state pack sec 4) and client protection fund
   (sec 5). Get a lawyer if the amount is significant.
   No: continue.

3. Still representing you in an active matter?
   Yes: weigh ending the representation before you escalate; consider whether
   you have successor counsel lined up. Then continue.
   No: continue.

4. Have you asked in writing for an itemized bill and an explanation?
   No: send templates/01_itemized_bill_request.md, give 14 days.
   Yes, and they answered: go to 5.
   Yes, and they ignored you (10+ days): if state pack sec 4 names an
   assistance/intake track, contact it; if not, file the underlying complaint
   directly using the path your later answers point to. Then go to 5.

5. What is the core problem?
   Amount too high, or charged for things not agreed to: go to 6.
   Cannot get your file or documents: send templates/03_file_request.md
   (Rule 1.16(d) or equivalent, state pack sec 1). Ignored: assistance track
   from Q4, or disciplinary authority sec 4 if no such track.
   Paid in advance and work not done: send templates/02_unearned_fee_refund.md.
   Refused, and no good-faith intent to perform: client protection fund, sec 5.
   Will not communicate, but bill is fine: written request first (Rule 1.4),
   then the assistance track from Q4 if your state has one.
   Something dishonest happened: disciplinary complaint. Go to 8.

6. Does your state have a fee arbitration program, and can the lawyer be
   forced into it? Read state pack sec 3, which answers both.
   No program exists: go to 7.
   Program exists, lawyer can decline: ask anyway, it is free. If declined,
   go to 7.
   Program exists, lawyer cannot decline: file this before a demand letter or
   small claims. Use sec 3 for who runs it, any dollar floor/ceiling, filing
   deadline, and fee.

7. Is the disputed amount within your state's small claims limit (sec 6)?
   Yes: send templates/04_demand_letter.md with a firm deadline. If refused,
   file in small claims per sec 6, in the right county or district; confirm
   the dollar figure with the clerk.
   No: talk to a lawyer about the general civil trial court in your state.

8. Do you want the conduct on the record regardless of money?
   Yes: disciplinary authority, state pack sec 4. Use
   templates/05_board_complaint_narrative.md. Choose "formal complaint" only
   for a serious ethical violation; if the real issue is bill size and your
   state pack names an informal or consumer-assistance track, choose that
   instead.
   No: done with the escalation path. Focus on the money route from above.
