# Decision tree

Printing one sheet to work from away from a screen? Use
`DECISION_TREE_ONE_PAGE.md` instead; this file is the authority it condenses.

Answer in order. Stop when you reach an action.

## 1. What state is the lawyer licensed and practicing in?

Nothing below this point works without this answer. The forums, the dollar limits,
the deadlines, and the program names all come from the state pack, not from this
file. Load `references/national_baseline.md` for the model rule shape, then load
`references/state_XX.md` for the two-letter code of that state. If you do not know
the state, ask before continuing.

## 2. Is money missing from a trust account, or did the lawyer take a retainer and do nothing at all?

Yes: this is potential dishonest conduct, not a fee dispute. File with the
disciplinary authority named in section 4 of your state pack, and read the client
protection or client security fund described in section 5 of your state pack. Get a
lawyer if the amount is significant.

No: continue.

## 3. Is the lawyer still representing you in an active matter?

Yes: decide whether you are ending the representation before you escalate. A dispute
with your current lawyer in the middle of a live case can hurt you. Consider whether
you have successor counsel lined up. Then continue.

No: continue.

## 4. Have you asked, in writing, for an itemized bill and an explanation?

No: do that first. Use `templates/01_itemized_bill_request.md`. Give fourteen days.
Almost nothing downstream works without this step.

Yes, and they answered: continue to 5.

Yes, and they ignored you: after ten days with no response, check section 4 of your
state pack. If it names a non-disciplinary assistance or intake track, contact that
office, which will follow up with the lawyer. If it does not, file the underlying
complaint or dispute directly using the path your later answers point you to. Then
continue to 5.

## 5. What is the core problem?

**The amount is too high, or I was charged for things I did not agree to.**

Go to 6.

**I cannot get my file or my documents.**

Send `templates/03_file_request.md`. Rule 1.16(d), or your state's equivalent
numbering, requires the lawyer to surrender papers and property you are entitled to.
Section 1 of your state pack confirms the numbering and whether your state departs
from it. If ignored, use the assistance track from question 4 if your state has one,
or the disciplinary authority in section 4 if it does not.

**I paid in advance and the work was not done.**

Send `templates/02_unearned_fee_refund.md`. If refused and the pattern suggests no
good faith intention to perform, read the client protection fund section, section 5
of your state pack.

**They will not talk to me, but the bill is fine.**

This is a Rule 1.4 communication problem. Written request first, then the assistance
track from question 4 if your state has one.

**Something dishonest happened.**

Disciplinary complaint. Go to 8.

## 6. Does your state have an attorney fee arbitration program, and does it bind your lawyer?

Read section 3 of your state pack. It answers both questions: whether a program
exists at all, and whether the lawyer can be forced into it or can simply decline.

**No program exists.** Go to 7.

**A program exists, but the lawyer can decline it.** Ask anyway, because it is free
and it may resolve the dispute even where the lawyer is not required to participate.
If the lawyer declines, go to 7.

**A program exists, and the lawyer cannot decline it.** This is your strongest first
move. Filing forces a resolution in a way no other forum here can. File before you
send a demand letter or go to small claims. Use section 3 of your state pack for who
runs it, any dollar floor or ceiling, any filing deadline, and any fee.

## 7. Is the disputed amount within your state's small claims limit?

Read section 6 of your state pack for the court's name and the dollar limit. Some
states set one statewide limit and some vary it by county or district, so confirm
the figure with the clerk either way.

Yes: send `templates/04_demand_letter.md` with a firm deadline. If it is refused,
file in the small claims equivalent named in section 6, in the right county or
district.

No: the amount is beyond small claims. Talk to a lawyer about the general civil
trial court in your state.

## 8. Do you want the conduct on the record regardless of money?

Yes: file with the disciplinary authority named in section 4 of your state pack.
Use `templates/05_board_complaint_narrative.md` to build the narrative. Choose
"formal complaint" only if you are alleging a serious ethical violation. If your real
issue is the size of a bill, and your state pack names an informal or
consumer-assistance track, choose that instead, because a billing disagreement filed
as a formal complaint usually ends in a closed file rather than a refund.

No: you are done with the escalation path. Focus on the money route you picked
above.
