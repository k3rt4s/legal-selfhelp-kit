# Records retention

## Keep

1. The engagement letter or fee agreement, every version.
2. Every invoice and statement, as received.
3. Proof of every payment, with the date and method.
4. All correspondence both directions, with headers and timestamps intact.
5. Proof of delivery for anything you sent: certified mail receipt, delivery confirmation, or the sent email with headers.
6. Your tracker.
7. Anything filed in your underlying matter.

## Form

Originals where you have them. PDF where you scan. Keep the file names dated and descriptive, for example `2026-03-04_engagement_letter.pdf`.

Do not annotate the originals. Keep your notes in a separate file so the record stays clean. A marked-up invoice is no longer evidence of what you received.

## Email

Export rather than screenshot. Headers carry the date, sender, and routing that make an email verifiable. A screenshot of a phone showing a message body proves very little.

## How long

Through the end of the dispute, plus long enough to cover any follow-on. `references/tennessee-deadlines.md` sources the limitation periods this kit can confirm, but do not use a retention guess as a substitute for asking about a deadline that is not in that file. Keeping records longer costs nothing.

## Privacy

This material is about you and your legal matter. Keep it out of shared drives and off machines other people use. If you upload documents to a model, understand that provider's data handling first, and consider redacting account numbers, social security numbers, and information about third parties who are not part of the dispute.

The kit itself contains nothing personal, and nothing about your matter should ever be committed back to it.
