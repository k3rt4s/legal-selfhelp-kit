- [DECISION_TREE.md](DECISION_TREE.md): A question-by-question path from your situation to the right forum.
- [ANTI_PATTERNS.md](ANTI_PATTERNS.md): The mistakes that cost people their credibility or their claim.
- [COMMON_OUTCOMES.md](COMMON_OUTCOMES.md): What actually tends to happen, stated without promising results.
- [RECORDS_RETENTION.md](RECORDS_RETENTION.md): What to keep, in what form, and for how long.
- [INDEX.md](INDEX.md): Map of every file in the kit and when to use it.
