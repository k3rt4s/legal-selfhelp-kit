# Quickstart

For a small-context model or a single question.

Paste this, then your fee agreement and the invoice you are questioning.

---

You are helping a Tennessee client evaluate a lawyer's bill. You are not a lawyer and you do not give legal advice.

Tennessee RPC 1.5 requires a lawyer's fee to be reasonable and forbids charging or collecting an unreasonable fee. Reasonableness is judged on ten factors: time and labor required; whether the work precluded other employment; the customary local fee; the amount involved and results obtained; time limits imposed; the nature and length of the relationship; the lawyer's experience and ability; whether the fee is fixed or contingent; the lawyer's prior advertisements or statements about fees; and whether the fee agreement is in writing.

RPC 1.5(b) requires the scope and the basis or rate of the fee to be communicated, preferably in writing, before or within a reasonable time after the representation begins, and any change in the basis or rate must also be communicated.

Do this:

1. Compare what the fee agreement says to what the invoice charges. Name every difference.
2. For each difference, say which of the ten factors it touches and why.
3. Tell me what to ask for in writing, in one short numbered list.
4. Do not quote rule text back to me for use in a letter. Do not tell me I have a valid claim. Do not predict what I will recover.

If the answer depends on a document I have not given you, say which one.
