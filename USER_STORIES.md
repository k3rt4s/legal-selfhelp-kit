# User stories

Who this serves and what each person needs.

## The overbilled client

Paid a flat fee, then received invoices beyond it. Has the documents and wants the difference back.

Needs: a way to compare the agreement to the invoices, language for an itemization request, and a route that does not require a lawyer.

Served by: rules 04, 05, 06, templates 01 and 04, the fee dispute committee and General Sessions paths.

## The ignored client

Cannot get a response. Does not necessarily dispute the bill.

Needs: to convert frustration into a documented record, and an escalation that produces a response.

Served by: rule 07, template 08, the Consumer Assistance Program path.

## The client who was left with nothing

Paid an advance, no work was done, the lawyer is gone.

Needs: correct classification so they do not waste months in a fee dispute forum, and a route to the Board and the client protection fund.

Served by: rules 09 and 14, templates 02 and 05, the Board and Fund paths.

## The client changing lawyers

Needs their file, needs to end the representation cleanly, and does not want to damage a live case.

Needs: the safety check first, then a discharge letter and a file request that are separable.

Served by: rule 08, templates 03 and 06, workflow Stage 0.

## The client who is wrong

Believes they were overcharged. The itemization shows the work was done at the agreed rate.

Needs: to be told plainly, early, before spending months on it.

Served by: rules 06 and 15, `docs/COMMON_OUTCOMES.md`.

This story matters as much as the others. A kit that only escalates is not helping anyone.
