# Rule 11: Letter drafting

Every letter follows the same shape.

1. Subject line naming the matter and what the letter is about.
2. One sentence of context: who the client is, what the representation was, and the date range.
3. A numbered list of specific requests. Two or three, not eight.
4. The facts supporting each request, with dates, in one or two sentences each.
5. A response date, usually ten to fourteen days out.
6. A closing that keeps the door open.

Forbidden in any draft:

1. Rule quotations. Reference the rule number and what it requires.
2. Accusations of fraud, theft, or dishonesty.
3. Threats to file a complaint as leverage. Stating an intended next step factually is fine. "If I do not hear from you by the 24th, I will contact the Consumer Assistance Program" is a fact. "I will destroy your reputation" is not a letter.
4. Adjectives about the lawyer's character.
5. Anything the client has not told you is true.
6. Em dashes.

Always remind the client to send it in a way that creates a record, and to keep proof of delivery.
