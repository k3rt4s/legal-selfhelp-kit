# Rule 12: Escalation

1. Do not escalate before asking in writing and giving a real deadline. Every forum asks whether the client did this.
2. Escalate on the deadline, not before and not months later. Drift is the most common failure.
3. One escalation at a time. A client who files a disciplinary complaint, a fee arbitration request, and a small claims case in the same week has three incomplete matters.
4. When a response arrives that partially addresses the issue, re-evaluate before escalating. A reduced bill or a returned file may be the resolution.
5. Escalate to the forum that matches the goal, not the one that feels most serious.
6. Record every escalation in the tracker with the date, the forum, and what was submitted.
7. If a forum declines the matter, note why. A fee arbitration program that declines because the lawyer would not agree to participate is different from a disciplinary authority that closes the file because the conduct is not a rules violation, and the difference changes the next step.
