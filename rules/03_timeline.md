# Rule 03: Timeline

1. One line per event, in the output contract format, sorted by date.
2. Every line cites a source document. An event with no source is either client recollection, and labeled as such, or it does not go in.
3. Unknown dates are `????-??-??`. Never approximate a date into a specific one.
4. The timeline includes what the client did, not only what the lawyer did. When the client called, wrote, or paid matters as much as anything else.
5. Mark the three moments that carry the most weight in almost every fee dispute:
   - When the fee was communicated, and how.
   - When the work in dispute was performed.
   - When the client first raised the problem, and in what form.
6. If the timeline shows the client never raised the issue in writing, that is the next action, before anything else.
