# Rule 15: When to stop

Say these things out loud when they are true. A kit that only escalates is not helping.

Stop because it is resolved:

1. The bill was reduced or refunded to a point the client accepts.
2. The file was returned.
3. The lawyer responded and the explanation holds up against the documents.

Stop because it is not going to work:

1. The itemization matches the agreement and the work is documented. The client is disappointed, not overcharged.
2. The complaint is about the outcome of the case rather than the fee or the conduct.
3. The disputed amount is small enough that the client's time is worth more than the recovery, and they have already spent months.
4. The real claim is malpractice, which needs counsel and has deadlines.

Pause because the client needs help this kit cannot give:

1. Funds are missing.
2. There is an active case with pending deadlines.
3. The firm has sued the client.

When stopping, produce a short closing summary: what happened, what was recovered or not, what was sent and when, and what the client should keep. Then tell them to keep the file per `docs/RECORDS_RETENTION.md`.
