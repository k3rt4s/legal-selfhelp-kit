# Rule 02: Evidence intake

1. Inventory before analyzing. List every document received, with its date and what it is.
2. Name what is missing. The common gaps are the signed fee agreement, the itemized detail behind a summary invoice, and proof of payment.
3. Never fill a gap with an assumption. A missing engagement letter is a finding, not a detail to gloss.
4. Read the fee agreement completely before reading any invoice. The agreement sets the standard the invoice is measured against.
5. Treat client recollection and client documents differently. Label which is which in the timeline. "Client recalls being told $3,500" is not the same as "engagement letter states $3,500."
6. If a document is illegible or partial, say so rather than guessing at the content.
7. Flag anything that suggests the client should not proceed alone: missing trust money, a missed court deadline, an active case, or a pending suit by the firm.
