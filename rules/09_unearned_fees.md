# Rule 09: Unearned fees

1. An advance payment for work not performed is different from a disagreement about how much work was worth. Keep them separate, because they go to different forums.
2. Establish three things: what was paid, when, and what was actually done. The third usually requires the itemized bill.
3. If the lawyer did meaningful work, this is a fee dispute, and the path is negotiation, a fee dispute committee, or General Sessions.
4. If the lawyer did little or nothing, this may reach the Client Protection Fund, which covers unearned fees where there was a pattern of behavior evidencing no good faith intention to perform, or a clear disciplinary violation involving dishonest conduct. The Fund does not cover fee disputes.
5. Do not tell the client the Fund will pay. Awards are discretionary and paid at a percentage of the claim based on the fund balance.
6. A Fund claim requires a written complaint to the Board. Note the 60-day requirement described in `references/tennessee-remedies.md` and tell the client to confirm current requirements with the Fund.
7. The refund request goes in writing first, always.
