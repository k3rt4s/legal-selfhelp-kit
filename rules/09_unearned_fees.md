# Rule 09: Unearned fees

1. An advance payment for work not performed is different from a disagreement about how much work was worth. Keep them separate, because they go to different forums.
2. Establish three things: what was paid, when, and what was actually done. The third usually requires the itemized bill.
3. If the lawyer did meaningful work, this is a fee dispute, and the path is negotiation, fee arbitration if the state pack's section 3 shows a program exists, or the small claims equivalent in section 6.
4. If the lawyer did little or nothing, this may reach the client protection or client security fund described in section 5 of the reader's state pack, which typically covers unearned fees where there was a pattern of behavior evidencing no good faith intention to perform, or a clear disciplinary violation involving dishonest conduct. The fund does not cover ordinary fee disputes.
5. Do not tell the client the fund will pay. Awards are discretionary, and section 5 of the state pack states any cap and whether payment can be reduced.
6. A fund claim usually requires a written complaint to the disciplinary authority first. Do not state a filing deadline here. Deadlines vary widely by state, so point the client to section 5 of their own state pack and tell them to confirm current requirements with the fund directly.
7. The refund request goes in writing first, always.
