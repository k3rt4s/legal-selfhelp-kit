## Gap list

1. No confirmed statewide filing fee figure for Florida small claims. Commercial fee-guide
   sites (for example trysmallclaims.com) quote tiered figures roughly $55 to $300, but no
   primary or official schedule was reached. The pack points the reader to the county clerk
   instead of stating a number. Filing fees for small claims are set by county-level
   administrative order rather than the statewide rules, so the Small Claims Rules themselves,
   read directly in this review, do not contain a fee figure to confirm against; search stopped
   without locating a single statewide fee schedule.
2. Whether an appeal from a small claims judgment to circuit court is heard on the existing
   record or as a new trial was not confirmed against a primary source. The current Small Claims
   Rules were read directly in this review; Rule 7.230 states only that an appeal proceeds "in
   accordance with the Florida Rules of Appellate Procedure," without itself saying record or
   trial de novo. Search stopped without a direct, current reading of Fla. R. App. P. 9.030(c);
   only secondary rule-reproduction sites described this, and those were not used.
3. Whether Florida has a filing deadline for a bar disciplinary complaint was not confirmed
   either way. Search stopped after reading Chapter 14 (fee arbitration and grievance mediation)
   and the ACAP consumer page; the disciplinary procedure rules governing formal complaints
   (a different chapter of RRTFB) were not fetched in this session.
4. Whether Florida has a disciplinary restitution mechanism, separate from the Clients'
   Security Fund, that can order a lawyer to return money as part of a disciplinary case, was
   not researched. Search was not attempted beyond Chapter 7 (the Fund) and Chapter 14 (fee
   arbitration and grievance mediation).
5. No claim about fee forfeiture as a remedy for a serious breach of duty. This is a real
   doctrine in some states but was not researched for Florida, and the pack does not mention it
   rather than guess at its availability or scope.

## How to extend this file

Any new rule, deadline, dollar amount, phone number, or program name added to
[state_fl.md](state_fl.md) gets a row in one of the tables above, with its source and
retrieval date, in the same commit that adds it. A claim without a row is a defect.
