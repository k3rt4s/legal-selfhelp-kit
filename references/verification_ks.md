## Gap list

1. Sedgwick County and Topeka Bar Association fee dispute committees: whether the process is
   mandatory on the lawyer, any dollar ceiling, any filing deadline, and any filing fee, for
   both committees. This pass went well beyond a phone number for both: it identified the
   administering body for each (Wichita Bar Association for Sedgwick County, Topeka Bar
   Association's own panel for Shawnee County) and confirmed Topeka's process is non-binding
   on both sides from the association's own published form. Neither association's published
   material states whether the lawyer must participate, a dollar limit, a deadline, or a fee,
   and no further published procedural rules for either committee were located.

2. Whether Kansas's Rule 212 Attorney Diversion Program can include a fee-refund or
   restitution condition. The current rule text, as fetched, contains no such term. One case
   annotation from before the 2021 renumbering described restitution imposed as a form of
   discipline in a single historical matter under the prior rule numbering; this is not current
   diversion-program authority and was not used as a basis for any claim in the pack.

3. Any statutory deadline for filing a bar complaint with the Office of the Disciplinary
   Administrator. Nothing in the material fetched (the complaint form, the ODA landing page, or
   Rule 212) stated a deadline or affirmatively stated that none exists. Left out of section 4.

4. County-level or district-level variation in the $10,000 small claims jurisdictional limit.
   K.S.A. 61-2703(a) states the limit as a single statewide figure; no source describing a
   county-specific variation was found or expected, and none is claimed.

5. Whether any Kansas local bar association outside Sedgwick and Shawnee counties runs a
   comparable fee dispute committee. This pass searched for one and found none; the pack states
   that finding as the current picture rather than as a certainty that no other county program
   exists anywhere in the state.
