## Gap list

1. No specific filing deadline for an FDR Program petition, such as a number of years from the
   bill, the payment, or the end of the representation, was found. The FDR Program pages, FAQ,
   "All about" article, and the petition form itself were all reviewed; none states a filing
   deadline. The form does ask when the attorney last provided services, suggesting the
   Committee may consider timing case by case, but no stated deadline was found.
2. No dollar ceiling on the size of a dispute the FDR Program will hear was found, only the
   $500 floor confirmed above.
3. Direct fetches of several courts.mo.gov Rule 4 pages failed intermittently during this
   session with a connection-refused error on the bare courts.mo.gov host, while the
   www.courts.mo.gov host and the same content under an alternate URL path generally succeeded.
   Every claim that had rested on a search-result summary was re-sourced this session to the
   Supreme Court order itself, fetched directly at the www host, so nothing in this pack now
   rests on a search summary. Use the www prefix on this domain.
4. Whether OCDC has any deadline for filing a complaint was not found; OCDC's own pages describe
   the filing process without stating a time limit, but no page was found affirmatively stating
   that no deadline exists (unlike some other states' disciplinary counsel FAQs, which say so
   directly). This pack does not claim a deadline, and does not claim its confirmed absence.
5. Whether Missouri has a formal disciplinary restitution mechanism, letting OCDC or the
   Supreme Court order a lawyer to return client money as part of the discipline process itself,
   was not confirmed. A search turned up only a reinstatement condition requiring that persons
   injured by a disbarred or suspended lawyer's conduct have received restitution or been
   notified of the reinstatement application, which is a precondition to the lawyer getting
   their license back, not a restitution order obtained through filing a complaint. This
   distinction was not sourced closely enough to state as a pack claim either way.
6. No separate lifetime or multi-claim aggregate cap on Client Security Fund payouts tied to a
   single lawyer's overall misconduct, distinct from the per-single-trust cap in Article 3 of
   the Fund's rules, was found.
7. The Missouri small claims court filing fee was not found in the statutes reviewed (RSMo
   482.300 through 482.365). Unlike some states, no statewide fee figure appears in the chapter
   itself; the pack states that the fee is generally set and collected locally and should be
   confirmed with the county circuit clerk.
8. A statute of repose for legal malpractice, an outer limitations period running regardless of
   discovery, was not found in the RSMo 516 series sections reviewed for this pack.
9. Whether a Rule 4 violation is admissible as evidence of the standard of care in a Missouri
   legal malpractice case was not researched. The kit does not cover malpractice strategy.
10. Any typical dollar recovery from the FDR Program, a bar complaint, or small claims court was
    not sought; no verified dataset exists for this and none is claimed in the pack.
11. County-level or local-bar fee dispute committees separate from the statewide Missouri Bar
    FDR Program were not surveyed. Given the FDR Program's statewide scope, this pack treats
    the statewide program as the relevant forum and did not search for local alternatives.
12. Whether the Fund's $50,000-per-claim cap and $50,000-per-trust cap have been amended since
    the Committee's rules were last amended (November 15, 2018, per the document's own
    amendment history) was not independently checked against any more recent amendment;
    nothing newer than that date was found.

## How to extend this file

Add a row to the confirmed table for any new claim, with its source URL and retrieval date,
before adding the claim to state_mo.md. If the best available source is not primary or
official, add the claim to the flagged table with a reason instead. If no adequate source can
be found, add the item to the gap list and leave it out of the pack.
