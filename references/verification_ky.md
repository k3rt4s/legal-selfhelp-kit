## Gap list

1. No published filing deadline or window was found for requesting KBA Legal Fee Arbitration
   Panel review (for example, a set number of days from the final bill or the end of the
   representation). The KBA's Dispute Resolution page and the Agreement to Arbitrate form
   describe the process but do not state a filing deadline. The pack tells the reader not to
   wait, since the statutes of limitation in section 7 keep running regardless.

2. No published deadline for filing a bar complaint against a Kentucky lawyer was found on the
   KBA's File a Complaint page. The pack notes this as unconfirmed rather than stating there is
   no deadline.

3. No specific dollar filing fee for Kentucky's Small Claims Division was found in either the
   AOC Small Claims Handbook (P-6) or KRS 24A.200/24A.230. The handbook references "a filing
   fee" multiple times without stating the amount. The pack tells the reader to confirm the
   current fee with their county circuit court clerk.

4. Case law drawing the line between a KRS 413.245 professional-services claim and an ordinary
   contract claim over legal fees (see Abel v. Austin, 411 S.W.3d 728 (Ky. 2013), and the more
   recent Emery Law Office Inc. v. Joel Franklin, 2026) was identified via WebSearch only and
   never independently fetched from a primary court opinion. Both are recorded in the Flagged
   table above rather than cited as confirmed holdings in the pack.

5. Whether Kentucky's disciplinary process has ever ordered restitution as a condition of
   probation in a specific case (as opposed to the general disciplinary sanctions described on
   the KBA's complaint page) was not independently confirmed against a primary Kentucky
   Supreme Court disciplinary order. The pack states plainly that no general restitution
   mechanism was found, without ruling out an individual case-by-case probation condition.

6. A general description of the Kentucky Bar Association's own institutional role (for
   example, a stated total membership count) was not sourced, since no specific claim
   requiring that figure appears in the pack. If a future revision adds membership figures or
   similar KBA institutional statistics, they will need their own sourced row.
