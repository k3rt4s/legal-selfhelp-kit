# Verification status of every citation in state_ne.md

Every claim in state_ne.md is listed here with where it came from, when it was retrieved, and
how far it should be trusted. If a claim is not on this list, it is not sourced, and it should
not be in the pack. Retrieval date for everything below is 2026-08-20. This pass is an
independent re-verification of a prior draft: every URL was re-fetched this pass with a
browser-identified curl request and the retrieved body was grepped for the specific claim text;
a 200 status alone was never treated as confirmation. This pass found and corrected several
errors in the prior draft; each corrected row says so and states what was wrong.

## Corrections made this pass

1. Neb. Rev. Stat. section 7-108 does not say what the prior draft attached to it. The statute
   text itself covers only a lien on the client's papers and on money in the attorney's hands or
   in an adverse party's hands after notice. It says nothing about "books" or "documents" (that
   phrase comes from a 1900 case construing the statute, not the statute itself) and nothing
   about a client's right to release the lien by furnishing security. That release mechanism was
   fabricated or imported from another state's law; it is not in Nebraska's statute or its
   official case annotations. Corrected in state_ne.md section 2 and in the rows below.
2. The prior draft cited Neb. Rev. Stat. section 25-2802 for the small claims attorney-
   representation bar and the two-per-week, ten-per-year filing cap. Both provisions are
   actually in section 25-2803 ("Parties; representation"), subsections (2) and (6). Section
   25-2802 is the jurisdictional dollar-amount statute only and does not mention attorneys or
   filing limits at all. The draft's citation to section 25-2804 for the representation bar was
   also wrong; section 25-2804 covers how a claim is commenced and mentions attorneys only for
   post-judgment motions. Corrected throughout.
3. The prior draft stated that a small claims appeal to district court is "a trial de novo."
   This is wrong. Section 25-2807 sends a small claims appeal through the general county-court
   appeal procedure in sections 25-2728 to 25-2738, and section 25-2733 states in so many words
   that the district court reviews the case "for error appearing on the record," not as a new
   trial. A 2024 Nebraska Supreme Court decision confirms this applies to small claims appeals.
   Corrected in state_ne.md sections 6 and the summary list.
4. The prior draft's gap list said no statute of repose was found for professional negligence
   claims under section 25-222, calling it an unconfirmed gap. This was wrong: the statute's own
   text states a ten-year repose period in plain words, and the statute's official annotations
   cite a Nebraska Supreme Court case holding that period constitutional. This is now stated as
   confirmed in state_ne.md section 7, not left as a gap.
5. The prior draft's gap list said Article 9 (trust account rules) could not be reached because
   two individual rule pages 404'd. The 404s were real, but the cause was a wrong URL slug
   format, not a missing rule. The correct URLs were found from the Article 9 index page's own
   links and fetched successfully this pass. See the closed-gap note below.
6. state_ne.md's own inline citation for the grievance-filing page pointed to
   <https://supremecourt.nebraska.gov/attorney-discipline/file-grievance>, which returns a 404.
   This did not match the working URL already used in the prior verification file. Corrected to
   the working URL.
7. Rule 3-501.5(f)'s description was accurate but incomplete: the rule states specific minimum
   contents for the accounting (itemized hourly charges, costs, interest, past-due balances, and
   a description of services performed and by whom for hourly work), and conditions the right on
   a "reasonable and timely request," not a bare request. Expanded in state_ne.md section 2.
