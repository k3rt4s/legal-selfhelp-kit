## Gap list

1. No claim that a Rules of Professional Conduct violation gives a client a private right of
   action in Tennessee. It does not, and the pack says so. This is stated on the strength of
   the rules' own scope and purpose (a disciplinary code, not a civil cause of action) rather
   than a single located statute or opinion saying so in as many words; no further search was
   done because the negative is well established in how the rules describe their own purpose.
2. Whether a Rules of Professional Conduct violation is admissible as evidence of a standard
   of care in a Tennessee malpractice case was not researched. The kit does not cover
   malpractice strategy, so this was not pursued past the limitation-period deadlines needed to
   tell a reader a clock exists.
3. No claim about the Tennessee Consumer Protection Act applying to a lawyer's professional
   services. Tennessee case law on that question is not settled in the way consumer kits often
   assume, and it remains unresearched.
4. No dollar figure for what any particular remedy typically recovers. There is no verified
   dataset behind such a number, and none was sought.
5. Filing fee for a General Sessions case was not located as a statewide figure; the pack
   points the reader to the county clerk instead of stating a number, because the fee varies by
   county and search stopped at the County Technical Assistance Service's jurisdiction page,
   which does not itself list fees.
6. Filing deadlines for the Bristol, Kingsport, Montgomery County, and Washington County fee
   dispute programs were not confirmed. Search stopped at the Board of Professional
   Responsibility's consolidated description of these programs, which lists their existence
   but not their procedural rules; each program's own rules page could not be located.
7. An effective date for the current version of Rule 8 as a whole, separate from the November
   30, 2022 amendment cutoff of the Tennessee Bar Association's 2023 Edition, was not located.
   Search stopped where tncourts.gov became unreachable; see the flagged table above.

## How to extend this file

Any new rule, deadline, dollar amount, phone number, or program name added to
[state_tn.md](state_tn.md) gets a row in one of the tables above, with its source and
retrieval date, in the same commit that adds it. A claim without a row is a defect.
