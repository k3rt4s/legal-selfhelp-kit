## Gap list

1. No New Jersey statute of repose specific to legal malpractice was located. This session
   searched the full text of the New Jersey Legislature's own official bulk statutes feed
   (`STATUTES.TXT`, `https://pub.njleg.gov/statutes/STATUTES-TEXT.zip`) for "legal malpractice"
   and for "repose" across the entire body of state statutes, not just Title 2A. The only
   "repose" hits are an unrelated 10-year construction statute of repose (N.J.S.A. 2A:14-1.1)
   and affordable-housing "judgment of repose" provisions that have nothing to do with legal
   malpractice. N.J.S.A. 2A:14-1 itself states only a six-year period running from accrual, with
   no outer repose figure. Search stopped there.
2. No filing deadline for an ethics grievance with the Office of Attorney Ethics was found. The
   OAE's own "File an Ethics Grievance" page describes the review and investigation process but
   states no time limit. Search stopped at that page.
3. No New Jersey disciplinary restitution mechanism, separate from the Lawyers' Fund for Client
   Protection, was found. The grievance page describes a nonpublic "diversion" program for minor
   matters, which corrects conduct rather than returning money. Search stopped at that page.
4. Whether a corporate party must be represented by counsel in the Special Civil Part or the
   Small Claims Section was not confirmed. Search stopped at the two self-help pages used for
   Section 6, which do not address corporate representation.
5. Whether either side may seek a trial de novo or otherwise appeal a Special Civil Part or
   Small Claims judgment, and any related time limit, was not researched. It was out of scope
   for the sourcing done this session.
6. No county-by-county variation in the Small Claims or Special Civil Part jurisdictional limits
   was found; both self-help pages describe the limits as statewide with no county exception
   named. This is a negative finding, not an unresearched gap.
7. Whether written contracts and oral contracts are treated differently under N.J.S.A. 2A:14-1
   was not confirmed; the statute text read this session applies the same six-year period to a
   contractual claim or liability "express or implied," without a separate oral-contract period,
   but a case-law gloss on that distinction was not researched.
8. The Supreme Court's *Delaney v. Dickey*, 244 N.J. 466 (2020), decision was not read directly
   this session; the pack's description of it rests entirely on the Official Comment to RPC 1.4
   in the flagged table above, which itself may not capture the full holding.
9. Whether New Jersey has local variations or supplemental fee-dispute procedures beyond the 17
   statewide District Fee Arbitration Committees was not researched beyond the official fee
   arbitration page, which describes one statewide program rather than local programs, unlike
   Tennessee's local-committee structure.

## How to extend this file

Any new rule, deadline, dollar amount, phone number, or program name added to
[state_nj.md](state_nj.md) gets a row in one of the tables above, with its source and
retrieval date, in the same commit that adds it. A claim without a row is a defect.
