## Gap list

1. Whether Idaho case law recognizes a rule violation as admissible evidence of the standard
   of care in an actual malpractice case, and how courts have applied that in practice, was
   not researched. The IRPC Scope section states the possibility in the abstract; confirming
   how Idaho courts have used it would require a case-law search, which is outside this pack's
   sourcing method (official rule and statute text, not case law) and outside this kit's scope
   (malpractice strategy). Left out of section 8 rather than asserted.
2. Whether Idaho has a statutory contingent-fee percentage cap or sliding scale outside the
   IRPC, for example in a specific practice area such as medical malpractice, was checked only
   within the IRPC text itself (Rule 1.5), where none was found. A broader search of the Idaho
   Code outside Title 1, 5, 7, and 12 for such a cap was not performed. Left out of section 2
   rather than asserted as confirmed absent.
3. Whether any Idaho court or bar publication states a formal notice requirement for a lawyer
   to tell a client about the right to fee arbitration before the lawyer sues for fees was not
   found in Idaho Bar Commission Rules 700-708 or on the Bar's fee arbitration page. The pack
   states this as "not found" rather than affirmatively "does not exist," since a requirement
   could in principle sit elsewhere and was not exhaustively searched for.
4. RESOLVED this pass. The small claims filing fee ($69.00 total) and the appeal filing fee
   ($81.00 total) were both sourced to a fee schedule PDF marked effective July 12, 2024. This
   pass confirmed, by fetching the live Idaho Rules of Civil Procedure page directly, that the
   court's own current rules page still links that same July 12, 2024 schedule as of the
   retrieval date above. A reader should still confirm no newer schedule has since superseded
   it, since court fee schedules are revised periodically and this pack cannot monitor that on
   an ongoing basis.
5. courts.idaho.gov and several other court-hostname variants (www.isc.idaho.gov, idcourts.us,
   www.courts.idaho.gov) either returned 403 Forbidden or failed to resolve during this
   research pass. Only the bare isc.idaho.gov hostname worked. If isc.idaho.gov is restructured
   in the future, a later verification pass should retest these alternate hostnames rather than
   assuming the site is unreachable.
