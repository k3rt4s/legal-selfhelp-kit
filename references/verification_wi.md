## Gap list

1. Looked for the Wisconsin Lawyers' Fund for Client Protection's current aggregate cap per dishonest attorney (as distinct from the per-claimant cap above). SCR 12.08(2)(a) authorizes the committee to set this by its own rule, but that committee rule was not found published on wicourts.gov or wisbar.org. The 2009 Inside Track article covers only the per-claimant figure. Search stopped after checking SCR chapter 12 itself and the wisbar.org public-facing Client Protection Fund pages; no aggregate figure was located. This is omitted from the pack.
2. Looked for a filing deadline specific to requesting fee arbitration under the State Bar's Fee Arbitration Program (as opposed to the 30-day dispute-response window that applies only when SCR 20:1.5(g) governs the advance fee). Checked the full 2022 Fee Arbitration Program rules, the "About the Fee Arbitration Program" PDF, and the "Procedural Overview for Fee Arbitration" PDF, all from wisbar.org. None state a limitations period for filing an application. The pack notes this as an open question rather than asserting a deadline.
3. Looked for whether Wisconsin's small claims dollar limits or filing procedures vary by county beyond the statewide figures in Wis. Stat. 799.01 and 814.62. Chapter 799 as fetched sets statewide limits; no county-level variation provision was found in the chapter text itself, and no county-by-county comparison was attempted. The pack notes this was not confirmed rather than asserting uniformity beyond the statute's own terms.
4. Looked for a Wisconsin statute of repose specific to legal malpractice claims (as distinct from the discovery-rule case law noted in the Confirmed table). Wis. Stat. chapter 893's general repose provisions (for example 893.89, product liability) did not, on inspection of chapter 893's full text, name legal malpractice or professional services claims specifically. No malpractice-specific repose period was located. This is omitted from the pack; only the three-year discovery-rule period is stated.
5. Did not independently fetch or read the full text of the underlying court opinions in Acharya v. Carroll or Hennekens v. Hoerl. Both are cited only through Wisconsin's own official annotated statutes (docs.legis.wisconsin.gov), which this kit treats as a primary source for the existence and general holding of each case, but the opinions themselves were not confirmed via CourtListener or another case database, so no separate flagged row was added; the Confirmed row above states this limitation directly instead.
6. Did not attempt to fetch or verify State Bar of Wisconsin Formal Ethics Opinion E-95-4 (1995), the retaining-lien opinion cited in the Wisconsin Committee Comment to SCR 20:1.16. Only the SCR comment's own citation to it was read. The opinion itself may not have a stable public URL; this was not checked further within the scope of this task.
