## Gap list

1. Looked for a Wyoming State Bar ethics opinion specifically addressing whether a lawyer
   may withhold a client's file for unpaid fees, beyond what Rule 1.15A and W.S. 29-9-102
   already state. This kit did not complete a search of the Wyoming State Bar's ethics
   opinion archive at wyomingbar.org within the scope of this task. The pack does not
   claim an ethics opinion exists or does not exist; it states only what the rule and the
   statute say.
2. Looked for a fee schedule or court rule that expressly labels a dollar figure as the
   docket or filing fee for a circuit-court-to-district-court civil appeal, separate from
   the general district court civil filing fee statute. Read the Uniform Rules for the
   Circuit Courts of Wyoming, the Wyoming Rules of Civil Procedure for Circuit Courts, the
   main Wyoming Rules of Civil Procedure, the Uniform Rules for District Courts of
   Wyoming, and the Wyoming Rules of Appellate Procedure. None of these rule sets states
   an appeal-specific dollar figure; the pack's $160.00 figure is this kit's own reasoned
   cross-reference from WRAP Rule 2.09(a) to the district court civil filing fee statute,
   flagged above rather than stated as a directly confirmed appeal fee.
3. Looked for a case citation confirming Wyoming courts apply W.S. 1-3-107 to legal
   malpractice claims against attorneys specifically, not only medical malpractice
   claims. Not independently confirmed; see the flagged table above. The pack states the
   statute's plain-text reading only.
4. Looked at the two other, differently-named Client Protection Fund rule PDFs found on
   the same live wyocourts.gov index page as the current Rules for the Client Protection
   Fund: "Rules of Clients' Security Fund and Committee of the Wyoming State Bar" and
   "Rules of Procedure for the Clients' Security Fund of the State Bar of Wyoming." These
   were located by URL but not fetched or read this session. The current Rules for the
   Client Protection Fund PDF used for section 5 is dated 2017 and refers to itself by
   its current "Client Protection Fund" name throughout, which this kit treats as strong
   internal evidence that the two "Clients' Security Fund" PDFs are an earlier, superseded
   name for the same program, but this was not independently confirmed by opening and
   dating those two other documents. The pack cites only the 2017 Rules for the Client
   Protection Fund.
5. Looked for a Wyoming statute of repose or continuous-representation tolling rule
   specific to legal malpractice, separate from the general periods in W.S. 1-3-105 and
   1-3-107. The full text of Wyoming Statutes Title 1, Chapter 3 was read and no such
   provision was found. This is omitted from the pack; only the statutory limitation
   periods and the discovery-rule extension actually stated in W.S. 1-3-107 are included.
6. Looked for the current transcript or court reporter fee a party must pay to prepare
   the record for a circuit-to-district small claims appeal, separate from the district
   court docket fee. WRAP Rule 2.09(a) mentions this fee exists but does not state an
   amount, and no fee schedule with a specific figure was found within the scope of this
   task. The pack tells the reader this cost exists and is variable, without stating a
   figure.
7. Did not independently verify whether the two other, differently-titled circuit court
