Use when the rate billed does not match the rate you were told, or when it changed without notice.

Re: Billing rate discrepancy, [MATTER NAME]

Dear [LAWYER NAME]:

The fee agreement I signed on [DATE] provides for a rate of [AGREED RATE] for [WHOM]. [If applicable: I was also told on DATE that RATE would apply.]

The invoice dated [INVOICE DATE] charges [BILLED RATE] for [DESCRIBE THE ENTRIES]. I was not notified of a change in the rate before receiving this invoice.

I am requesting:

1. An explanation of the rate applied, and the date any change took effect.
2. A copy of any written notice of the change that you believe was provided to me.
3. A corrected invoice applying the agreed rate, or an explanation of why the agreed rate does not apply.

RPC 1.5(b) addresses communicating the basis or rate of a fee to a client and communicating any change in that basis or rate.

Please respond by [DATE].
