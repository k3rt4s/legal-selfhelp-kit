# Discharge letter

Do not send this during an active matter without knowing what is pending and, ideally, having successor counsel.

[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR ADDRESS]
[CITY, STATE ZIP]
[YOUR EMAIL] | [YOUR PHONE]

[DATE]

[LAWYER NAME]
[FIRM NAME]
[FIRM ADDRESS]
[CITY, STATE ZIP]

Sent by [certified mail, return receipt requested / email with delivery confirmation]

Re: Termination of representation, [MATTER NAME]

Dear [LAWYER NAME]:

I am writing to end your representation of me in the above matter, effective [DATE].

Please:

1. Take no further action on my behalf after the effective date, other than what is necessary to protect my interests during the transition.
2. Provide my complete client file by [DATE], including documents I provided, filings, correspondence, and work product prepared for me for which the firm has been paid.
3. Provide a final itemized statement covering all work through the effective date.
4. Refund any portion of my advance payment that has not been earned or incurred.
5. [If applicable] Coordinate with my successor counsel, [NAME, FIRM, CONTACT].
6. [If applicable] Tell me in writing what deadlines are pending in this matter in the next ninety days.

Rule 1.16(d) of the rules of professional conduct in this state addresses a lawyer's obligations on termination, including protecting the client's interests, surrendering papers and property, and refunding unearned advance payments.

Thank you for your work on this matter.

[YOUR NAME]
