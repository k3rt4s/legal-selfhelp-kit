# Fee arbitration submission

Read section 3 of your state pack (`references/state_XX.md`) before using this template. Whether you can use this at all, and what it will do for you, depends entirely on what your state runs.

**If your state pack says there is no fee arbitration program:** stop here. Do not submit this. Your state pack's section 3 will say so plainly. Use template 04, the demand letter, and your state's small claims court instead, named in section 6 of your state pack.

**If your state pack says the program is voluntary, and the lawyer can decline:** say so to yourself before you invest time in this. Submit the form below. If the lawyer declines to participate, your state pack's section 3 says what happens next; in most states that means the program cannot proceed and you fall back to template 04 and small claims court under section 6.

**If your state pack says the program is mandatory on the lawyer once you request it:** this is the strongest position a client can be in. The lawyer cannot simply refuse. Submit the form below to the body your state pack's section 3 names.

Every detail below, who to send this to, any filing fee, any dollar floor or ceiling, and any filing deadline, comes from section 3 of your state pack. Confirm the current form and process with that body before you rely on this.
