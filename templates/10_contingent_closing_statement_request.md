# Contingent fee closing statement request

At the conclusion of a contingent fee matter, the lawyer must provide a written statement showing the outcome and, if there was a recovery, the remittance to the client and how it was calculated. If you did not receive one, ask.

You represented me in [MATTER] under a contingent fee agreement dated [DATE]. The matter concluded on or about [DATE] with [OUTCOME].

I have not received a written closing statement. I am requesting:

1. The written statement of the outcome of the matter.
2. If there was a recovery, the amount, the fee taken, the expenses deducted, and the remittance to me, with the method of calculation shown.
3. Documentation for each expense deducted.

Rule 1.5(c) of the rules of professional conduct in this state addresses the written statement a lawyer must provide to a client at the conclusion of a contingent fee matter.
